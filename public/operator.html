→ The 1.5× markup covers what you're actually selling

  1. SES is raw plumbing — you're packaging it with template rendering, HMAC validation, flavour routing, retry logic, and monitoring. That's the real product.
  2. You're also absorbing development & maintenance time. The 50% gross margin is your hourly rate paid via volume, not a fee line item the client needs to see.
  3. Competitors charge $20–200/mo fixed (Klaviyo, Mailchimp). A usage-based model at $1.50 per 10k looks cheap to a Shopify merchant until volume is high — then it's still cheaper than Klaviyo at 200k+ sends.
  4. You keep pricing transparent: one line, one formula. Client trusts it. No hidden tiers, no "pro plan" gatekeeping.

→ Scaling logic

  1. The minimum viable scale to be worth your time is ~300k/mo. That's $15/mo margin, or $180/yr — still modest, but the system is running hands-off.
  2. At 1M emails/mo you're netting $50/mo = $600/yr. That's the first revenue milestone worth flagging to the client as a "you've grown" moment.
  3. At 5M+/mo you should consider raising the multiplier to 2× or introducing a flat base fee ($99/mo). Clients at that size are saving thousands vs Klaviyo — a $500/mo profit line is still underpriced for enterprise support.
  4. Cost stays perfectly linear — SES never throttles you with surprise pricing. No "email credits expire" nonsense.
