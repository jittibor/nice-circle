2 How the pipeline works

Shopify fires it,
we catch it, they receive it.

Five steps. Fully automatic. No human in the loop. No batch jobs. No queues. The moment Shopify registers the signup, the email is on its way.

STEP 01
🛒

Customer signs up

On your Shopify store — form, checkout opt-in, or dedicated landing page. Tagged "circle-club" + favourite flavour.

→
STEP 02
⚡

Webhook fires

Shopify's customers/create webhook instantly POSTs the customer data (HMAC-signed) to our endpoint.

→
STEP 03
🔐

Verify & route

HMAC signature check rejects forgeries. Customer tags decide which flavour template ships.

→
STEP 04
✉️

Render & send

Merge-tag substitution. SMTP dispatch via Gmail / SES. No API fees. No rate limits at our scale.

→
STEP 05
📬

In their inbox

End-to-end under 2 seconds. Customer sees their name, their flavour, their video — all pre-built on their behalf.
